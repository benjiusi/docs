## How tokens are grouped

Tokens are grouped by currency into one pool per currency:

| Pool | Tokens |
|---|---|
| USD | USDC, USDT, RLUSD, OUSD |
| EUR | EURC, EURAU, EURI |
| CHF | CHFAU |
| SGD | XSGD |
| JPY | JPYSC, JPYC |

Swaps inside a pool are 1:1 minus a fee. Swaps between pools use the oracle FX rate. See [Swaps](/swaps) and [How the exchange works](/how-it-works).

## Pool share tokens

Supplying to a pool mints its share token: `BFX-USD`, `BFX-EUR`, `BFX-CHF`, `BFX-SGD`, `BFX-JPY`. Share tokens grow in value as the pool earns fees. See [Earn](/earn).
