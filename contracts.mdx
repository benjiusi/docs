## How they fit together

```
Wallet ──signs──► Relay router (gasless, Permit2) ─┐
Wallet ──tx────► BlankFXRouterV4 ◄────────────────┘
                     │
                     ├── USD pool
                     ├── EUR / CHF / SGD / JPY pools  (oracle + pricing curve)
                     └── Payment router (payment links, point of sale)

Taker ──funds──► RFQ escrow ◄──fills── Market maker

BlankFXAccess + Risk engine: checked on trades and deposits
```

## Access tiers

Every wallet has an on-chain access tier:

| Tier | What it can do |
|---|---|
| Unverified | Swap and pay up to **$10,000 per rolling 30 days** without registering |
| Retail / Corporate | Verified wallets: no on-chain volume cap; can supply liquidity |
| Market maker | Required to fill RFQ quotes |

The app and API apply additional off-chain limits (for example, unverified payment value of $2,000 per day). See [Compliance & KYC](/compliance).

## Controls

- **Pause.** Each pool and the RFQ escrow can be paused by the admin in an incident. A paused pool stops swaps, deposits and withdrawals until it is unpaused.
- **Upgradeability.** The pools and the access contract are upgradeable proxies (UUPS); the routers are not upgradeable and are replaced by redeploying. A timelock on upgrades is planned before mainnet, so changes are announced before they take effect.
- **Parameter limits.** Fee and risk parameters can only be set within hard-coded bounds (for example, the treasury share of fees cannot exceed 50%, the transfer fee cannot exceed 0.50%).

## Audit

An external security audit is in progress. Findings raised so far have been fixed on Sepolia. Mainnet deployment waits for the audit to close.
