# Fees

The fee is always shown on the quote, before you confirm.

## Swap fees

| Swap | Base fee | Can rise to |
|---|---|---|
| Same-currency (e.g. USDC → USDT) | 0.05% | base + up to 1.50% when the pool is unbalanced |
| Cross-currency (e.g. USDC → EURC) | 0.10% | base + up to 1.50% when the pool is unbalanced |

The extra **inventory fee** applies when the coin you are buying is running short in its pool. It is zero while that coin's balance stays within a normal band and ramps up as the balance falls further. Buying a coin the pool holds plenty of costs the base fee.

On cross-currency swaps the rate itself also moves with size and pool balance (see [Swaps](/swaps#cross-currency-swaps)). That price movement is separate from the fee.

## Transfer fee

When a swap or payment delivers to **someone else's wallet** (a send, a payment link, a point-of-sale payment), a transfer fee of **0.05%** applies. Swaps back to your own wallet don't pay it.

## Where fees go

| Fee | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Swap fee | 80% to the pool's suppliers, 20% to the BlankFX treasury |
| Transfer fee | BlankFX treasury |

## Gas

- **Gasless swaps** (from $100, after a one-time Permit2 approval): BlankFX currently pays the network fee.
- **Swaps under $100, token approvals and deposits/withdrawals**: you pay normal Ethereum gas from your wallet.

## Earn

There is no fee to supply to or withdraw from a pool, and no performance fee on earnings. You pay only gas.

## Agent payments

An AI agent paying a payment link over x402 pays the invoice amount **plus the normal transfer fee, on top**. The merchant receives the full invoice amount. There is no separate agent fee. BlankFX pays the gas to submit the payment; the agent pays gas only for its one-time Permit2 approval. See [Agent payments](/agent-payments).

## RFQ

Market-maker quotes on RFQ are all-in prices. The RFQ escrow contract charges no fee.
