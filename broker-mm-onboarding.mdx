# Broker & Market-Maker Onboarding

To trade on BlankFX — providing RFQ liquidity or trading over the API — your firm goes through a one-time onboarding review. This page is the complete list of what we need; send it all in one package and we'll come back with anything missing rather than drip-feeding requests.

This is standard institutional-crypto onboarding (KYB), sized for a firm our stage can actually turn around quickly, not a bank's compliance program.

<Info>
  **Technical integration** (the endpoints you expose, how firm quotes are signed and how settlement works) is in [RFQ for market makers](/api-reference/rfq-market-makers). How pricing, oracles and pools work is in [How the exchange works](/how-it-works).
</Info>

<Info>
  **How much we ask depends on how you settle.** Most BlankFX flows settle on-chain and atomically — where that's true, our exposure to your firm is small and the checklist stays light. Delayed-settlement or credit-bearing flows carry real counterparty exposure, which is why the financial section below only applies to those.
</Info>

## What to send

### Corporate (KYB)

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Certificate of incorporation + registry extract | Recent |
| Constitutional document | Memorandum/articles or local equivalent |
| Ownership chart to UBOs ≥ 25% | Plus ID for each owner and director |
| Proof of registered/operating address | Recent |
| Authorized signatory evidence | Board resolution or equivalent naming who may bind the firm for trading |

### Regulatory & compliance

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Licenses/registrations | Where your activity requires one — or a signed statement that none is required, naming the jurisdiction |
| AML confirmation | Confirmation you run an AML program appropriate to your business + your compliance contact. Firms trading only their own capital: a short statement of that fact is enough |
| Sanctions declaration | No ownership/control by sanctioned persons, no operations in comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions, consent to ongoing screening |
| Litigation & enforcement | Anything material in the past 5 years, or a statement that there is none |

### Financial — delayed-settlement / credit-bearing flows only

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Atomic settlement">
    If you only trade atomically-settled flows, skip this section entirely — we set a conservative default exposure cap instead.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Delayed settlement">
    | Item | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Latest financial statements | Audited if available; management accounts are fine for younger firms |
    | Source of trading capital | Short statement — own capital, investor capital, or borrowings |
    | Capital adequacy | Confirmation of unencumbered capital comfortably above your requested exposure cap |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Operational & technical

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Settlement wallet address(es) | Each proven by signing a challenge message we provide |
| Named traders & key custodians | Who holds the API keys; keys are per-firm, never shared with sub-clients |
| Key management description | Where API/private keys live — HSM, MPC custodian, encrypted store |
| Ops & incident contacts | Trading-hours contact; 24/7 contact required for delayed-settlement flows |
| Expected activity profile | Pairs, typical clip sizes, expected daily volume — calibrates your limits, not a commitment |

### Agreements

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Signed liquidity-provider / trading agreement | Incorporates our API Terms and Prohibited Use Schedule |
| Fee/spread schedule acknowledgment | Countersigned term sheet |

<Info>
  **What we don't ask for:** bank references, notarized/apostilled documents (plain copies + a video call if we have identity doubts), proof-of-reserves of your firm, exclusivity, or volume minimums to get onboarded.
</Info>

## What happens after you submit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your package">
    Send everything above in one email, noting whether you expect atomic or delayed-settlement flows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Independent verification">
    Registry lookup of your entity, sanctions/adverse-media screening, on-chain history of your settlement wallets, and a license check against the relevant regulator's public register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test trade">
    A small supervised test trade before we raise your limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision">
    Roughly 1–2 weeks from a complete package; longer for firms needing enhanced review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## After you're onboarded

- Tell us **before** settling from a new wallet — unannounced addresses are rejected, not investigated after the fact.
- Notify us promptly of: a change of control or beneficial owners, loss of a relevant license, regulatory action against you, a key compromise, or insolvency events.
- We re-screen registry and sanctions status annually, and do a full refresh every couple of years.

Send your package to **trading@blankfx.org**. Include a single point of contact and whether you expect atomic or delayed-settlement flows.
