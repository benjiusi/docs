# FAQ

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is BlankFX?">
    BlankFX lets businesses get paid in any stablecoin and settle in the one they want. It converts stablecoins within and across currencies on-chain, with published fees and a full audit trail. See [What is BlankFX?](/introduction).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is BlankFX different from Uniswap?">
    Uniswap prices each pair from its own pool ratio. BlankFX holds one pool per currency: same-currency swaps (USDC → USDT) are 1:1 minus a fee, and cross-currency swaps start from an FX oracle rate with a pricing curve on top. Large cross-currency tickets can also be quoted firm by market makers over RFQ. See [How the exchange works](/how-it-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which networks are supported?">
    Sepolia (testnet) is live. Ethereum (mainnet) is planned.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can AI agents pay a BlankFX payment link?">
    Yes. A merchant who switches on agent payments lets any x402-compatible agent pay their payment links directly from the agent's own wallet, with no checkout page and no account. The agent pays the normal transfer fee on top of the invoice, and the merchant receives the full amount. See [Agent payments](/agent-payments) and the [developer guide](/api-reference/x402).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need ETH for gas?">
    Only for the one-time Permit2 approval of each token you sell, and for swaps under $100. Swaps of $100 or more are gasless: you sign a message and BlankFX submits the transaction and pays the network fee.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Earning

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do earnings come from?">
    From swap fees. 80% of every swap fee stays in the pool for its suppliers; 20% goes to the BlankFX treasury. There are no token emissions. See [Fees](/fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my deposit locked?">
    Newly supplied shares unlock after a cooldown: 5 hours in the USD pool, 5 minutes in the other pools. After that you can withdraw at any time, with no penalty. A pool that is paused during an incident blocks withdrawals until it is unpaused.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to claim rewards?">
    No. Fees accrue to the pool, so each pool share is redeemable for more over time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I lose money as a supplier?">
    Yes. The main risks are smart-contract bugs, a wrong or stale oracle, a coin in the pool losing its peg or being frozen by its issuer, and imbalance, where the pool is left holding more of a weak coin or currency. See [Earn → Risks](/earn#risks).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are the smart contracts audited?">
    An external audit is in progress, and the findings raised so far have been fixed on Sepolia. Mainnet deployment waits for the audit to close.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does BlankFX hold my funds?">
    No. Every swap, payment and deposit is signed by your wallet and settled by smart contracts. BlankFX never holds your private keys and cannot recover a lost wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a bug bounty?">
    A bug bounty is planned for the Ethereum (mainnet) launch. Until then, report vulnerabilities to security@blankfx.org.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
