# Swaps

## Two kinds of swap

| Swap | Example | How it is priced |
|---|---|---|
| **Same-currency** | USDC → USDT, EURC → EURAU | 1:1, minus a fee |
| **Cross-currency** | USDC → EURC, XSGD → USDC | Oracle FX rate, adjusted by a pricing curve, minus a fee |

Any listed token can be swapped for any other. The app picks the route; you don't choose pools. See [Supported tokens](/tokens) for the full list.

Every swap is **atomic**: it settles in full in one transaction or not at all. There are no partial fills.

## Same-currency swaps

Each currency has one pool holding all its stablecoins (for example, the USD pool holds USDC, USDT, RLUSD and OUSD). A same-currency swap exchanges one coin for another inside that pool at **1:1 minus the fee**.

The fee starts at the pool's base fee and rises when the coin you are buying is running short in the pool, which steers flow back towards balance. A pool never pays out a coin below 5% of what it owes in that coin. See [Fees](/fees).

## Cross-currency swaps

A cross-currency swap takes your coin into its currency pool and pays the other coin out of the other currency's pool. The rate:

1. **Starts from the oracle mid-rate** (e.g. EUR/USD). See [How the exchange works](/how-it-works#oracles) for oracle sources.
2. **Is adjusted by a pricing curve** that moves the rate against the trade as size grows or as the pool it pays out of gets short. Small trades in a balanced pool price very close to the oracle; large trades or depleted pools price further away.
3. **Has the fee deducted.**

If a quote would land more than **2% away from the oracle rate**, the pool refuses it. Larger tickets of that kind go to [RFQ](/api-reference/fx-flow), where market makers quote a firm price.

## Gasless swaps

The first time you sell a token, you approve it once to the canonical Permit2 contract. That approval is an on-chain transaction you pay gas for.

After that, swaps of **$100 or more** are gasless. You sign a message (free) and the BlankFX relayer submits the transaction through the relay router. BlankFX currently pays the network fee. The relayer cannot change your order: the contract only executes the exact amounts, tokens and recipient you signed.

Swaps under $100 are sent from your own wallet and you pay gas.

## Rate protection

Every swap carries a minimum output. If the rate moves against you by more than your tolerance between quote and execution, the transaction reverts and you keep your tokens.

The default tolerance is **0.10%**. You can set it between 0.01% and 1% under **Trade settings → Rate protection**.
